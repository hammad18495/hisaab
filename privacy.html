Hisaab privacy policy

Hisaab is a personal expense tracker. It runs entirely in your browser or phone and has no server of its own.

  • Where your data lives: every expense you record is written to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. Hisaab never copies your data to any other server.
  • Google access: after you sign in with Google, Hisaab uses Google Sheets and Google Drive access only to create, read and update your expenses sheet, to find sheets other people have shared with you through Hisaab, and to share your sheet with people you choose.
  • Sign-in: your name, email and profile photo are used only to show who is signed in and who owns a shared sheet.
  • On your device: the app keeps a short-lived sign-in token and a cached copy of recent months on your device so it opens quickly and works offline.
  • Sharing: a sheet is shared only when you choose to share it, and only with the email address you enter.
  • Removing access: sign out in the app, and revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
